## Privacy Policy for Virtuoso

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Virtuoso (the “Application,” “Service,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Virtuoso piano application for iOS.

Information We Collect
Virtuoso does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information.

The Application does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not collect or transmit your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, browsing history, advertising identifier, or other personal data.

App Data and Lesson Content
Virtuoso includes piano lessons, exercises, playback tools, scores, practice controls, and settings that operate on your device.

If you use the custom lesson import feature, the lesson entry you paste is processed locally by the Application. Custom lessons are temporary and remain available only for the current app session. They are cleared when the Application is reloaded or restarted. Virtuoso does not upload custom lesson content to us or store it on our servers.

You are responsible for ensuring that any music, MIDI files, lesson data, or other content you use with the Application complies with applicable laws and that you have the necessary rights or permissions to use it.

Network Access
Virtuoso’s bundled lessons, piano sounds, and core practice features are designed to work offline.

The Application may allow you to open an external lesson-converter website in your device’s browser. Visiting that website is optional and occurs outside Virtuoso. Any information you provide to that website is governed by the website operator’s own privacy policy and terms.

Virtuoso does not receive the MIDI files or other information you submit to an external website unless you independently choose to paste generated lesson text into the Application. Pasted lesson text is processed locally as described above.

Data Sharing
Because Virtuoso does not collect personal data, we do not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers, data brokers, business partners, or analytics providers.

We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a public authority. However, because the Application does not collect or maintain user accounts or personal data, we generally have no user data to disclose.

Apple Services
Apple may independently process information relating to App Store downloads, purchases, device diagnostics, or crash reports according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy policies. This information is controlled by Apple and is not collected directly by Virtuoso.

If Apple makes diagnostic or crash information available to the developer, it may be used only to identify errors, improve reliability, and maintain the Application. We do not use such information for advertising or tracking.

Data Retention and Deletion
Virtuoso does not maintain user accounts or remote user profiles.

Temporary custom lessons are removed when the Application is reloaded or restarted. You can remove locally stored Application data by deleting Virtuoso from your device. Some system-managed diagnostic or App Store information may remain under Apple’s retention practices.

Because we do not operate a server containing Virtuoso user profiles or personal data, there is ordinarily no personal account data for us to access, correct, export, or delete.

Security
Virtuoso is designed to minimize privacy risks by processing lesson and practice information locally and by avoiding unnecessary data collection.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. While we take reasonable measures to protect the Application, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy
Virtuoso does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Application does not require an account and does not ask users to submit personal information.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us using the details below so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Links to Other Websites
Virtuoso may contain links to websites that are not operated or controlled by us, including the optional lesson-converter website and music-source references.

When you open an external link, you leave Virtuoso. We are not responsible for the content, availability, security, privacy policies, or practices of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of every external service you use.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Virtuoso, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

When this policy changes, we will publish the revised version and update the “Last updated” date above. Changes become effective when the revised policy is posted.
